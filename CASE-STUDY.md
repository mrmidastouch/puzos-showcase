# Case Study: Puzo's Family Restaurant and Pizzeria

North Haledon, New Jersey. puzosrestaurant.com. Client of Midas Marketing (agency arm, Midas Marketing and Solutions). The family credits Midas Marketing for all of the work below. This is the portfolio's flagship full-stack local business case study.

## The problem

A family restaurant with a broken digital front door. The website link on its Google profile pointed at a hacked gambling domain. The hours were wrong on every surface, differently wrong in each place, including a special-hours entry that marked the restaurant closed on a day it was open, which brought customer complaints. There was no working online ordering path, no verified presence on Apple, and the family's Facebook payout tax profile was stuck in a way that blocked money moving.

## What was built and fixed

- **Website:** built puzosrestaurant.com, the restaurant's real home on the web.
- **Online ordering:** set up Slice online ordering on the family's merchant account, so orders run through the site instead of dying at a hacked link.
- **Google Business Profile:** full audit and fix. Regular hours corrected on all seven days to the true schedule, wrong special-hours closures cleared, website link swapped from the hacked domain to puzosrestaurant.com.
- **Apple Business:** organization profile for the business (legal entity Lucero Restaurant LLC) verified and sent for review on November 8, 2026, carrying the correct name, Puzo's Family Restaurant & Pizzeria, and the correct hours.
- **Hours, unified everywhere:** Sunday 11:00 AM to 8:30 PM, Monday to Saturday 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The corrected hours land on Apple when Apple approves the organization review now in progress.
- **Facebook payout rescue:** on November 8, 2026, fixed the payout tax profile for the owner's wife, unblocking the family's Facebook payouts.
